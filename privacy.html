Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026. The short version: the app collects nothing, and this website counts visits with a cookieless, no-tracking analytics tool.

The Qubiq app

Qubiq has no telemetry, no analytics, no crash reporting, no ads and no accounts — and no servers of ours, so there is nothing to breach and nothing to sell. The app makes network connections only to your VPN servers and the subscription providers you add.

  • Nodes, credentials, the HWID and settings live in the app's local storage on your device.
  • Optional logs are opt-in and never leave the device except when you share them yourself — the Send logs action produces a bundle with credentials redacted and your subscription URL removed before anything is written. Producing it re-requests that URL once, live, to dump the response status and headers.
  • The backup file is deliberately unencrypted and holds your credentials. It goes only to the iCloud Drive, OneDrive folder or file you bind yourself — keep that location private.

The user guide covers subscriptions, HWID and backup behaviour in detail.

This website

  • Hosting — this site is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub processes your IP address and request details to deliver pages; GitHub's privacy statement covers their side.
  • Analytics — we use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly, cookieless analytics service. It records that a page was viewed along with the referrer, browser, operating system and country — no IP address is stored, no cookies are set, and nothing follows you across sites.
  • Language — the site may open the version matching your browser language. If you choose EN/RU yourself, that choice is stored in this browser's storage; it never leaves your device.
  • No ads, no trackers, no cookies — which is why there's no consent banner: there is nothing to consent to.

Changes & contact

If this policy changes, the update lands on this page — that is the notification. Questions or requests: open an issue on the site repo.
