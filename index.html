Xray core · Private by design

Your servers. Your rules.
One client, every device.

Qubiq is a cross-platform Xray-core client for iOS, macOS, Android, Windows and Linux. It provides no VPN access — you bring your own servers or subscription, and it runs them everywhere.

No account, nothing phoned home

No ads, analytics, telemetry or crash reporting. Nodes and credentials stay in the app's local database — logs leave only when you share them.

For providers

Your panel, inside the app

Qubiq reads the subscription headers your panel already sends — and a few more. No SDK and nothing to register.

Your branding

Themes written for Happ's color-profile apply unchanged; a profile-logo puts your mark on the app bar and group card.

Plan & support

subscription-userinfo shows usage and expiry; announce, support-url and profile-web-page-url put notices and help on the group card.

Blocked domain? Move it

new-url and new-domain migrate subscriptions once the new address answers; fallback-domain adds a mirror for outages.

One-tap import

An Add to Qubiq button opens a prefilled form — nothing is saved until the user confirms.

Add your source

Paste share links, import an Xray JSON config, or add a subscription — Remnawave panels are picked up automatically.

Check latency and tap a row to pin it — or pin nothing and let automatic selection pick.

Press the button
